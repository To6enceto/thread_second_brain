    WHAT THREAD WILL READ

    Thread remembers this folder, what it may read, your projects folder and your AI model, in its own settings outside your notes. PDF permissions and saving are never remembered. Next time it shows them and reads nothing until you continue.

    Saving notes

    Thread can save answers, decisions and other notes into your folder. Saving is optional, and you always see the exact note before it is written.

    1. Close Obsidian and any other app that edits these notes.
    2. Choose Check the Obsidian plugin's files.
    3. If the check passes, choose Allow saving notes.

    Choose a notes folder first.

    Details

    Thread's own settings and recovery history stay in its profile, outside your notes. PDF permissions are never restored or imported, and read access returns only from saved access you chose to remember.

    The check reads only Thread's manifest.json, data.json, changes.json and project-imports.json in this settings folder (up to 40 MiB) and changes nothing. That history may contain earlier copies of notes; it is never searched or sent to AI.

    While saving is allowed, Thread holds an exclusive lock that a compatible Thread plugin also respects. Other editors are not covered, so keep them closed. Unknown or unresolved plugin recovery keeps saving off; reading always works.
